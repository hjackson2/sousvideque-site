The story

Two cooks, one steak.

Sous vide is precise but pale. A ripping-hot grill is gorgeous but easy to overshoot. SousVideQue puts them in order. The bath handles the inside; the fire handles the outside.

Every Short is one rule in the same shape: X first, Y second. Bath first, salt second. Sear first, slice second. Pan first, torch second. Learn the order, and the cook gets a lot calmer.

Food-safety note

Times and temps in our videos come from published sources, mainly the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service (FSIS) and tested sous vide guides such as Serious Eats. USDA lists 165F as the safe minimum internal temperature for poultry; lower sous vide temps rely on longer hold times from those tested guides. Always use a calibrated thermometer, follow the specific source for your cut and thickness, and when in doubt, check the original:

USDA FSIS safe temperature chart · Serious Eats sous vide guides

This page is general information, not professional food-safety advice.
